Privacy Policy for Scribe

Effective 13 June 2026 · Selah AI Consulting Limited (New Zealand)

The short version: Scribe is offline-first. There is no account to create, and we don't use analytics, tracking, or ads. We operate no servers and collect no personal data about you. Your reading data stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Scribe is published by Selah AI Consulting Limited, a company registered in New Zealand ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Scribe mobile app handles your information. If you have any questions, contact us at dev@aiconsult.co.nz.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Scribe — bookmarks, highlights, your reading position, and your settings — is stored only in local storage on your device. It never leaves your device, and we never see it or receive a copy. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

3. Purchases

The Scholar unlock is a one-time, non-subscription purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Restoring a purchase uses your Apple ID; we are not involved in and do not store that information.

4. AI Research feature (optional)

Scholar includes an optional AI Research feature. It is "bring your own key": you supply your own API key from an AI provider, and that key is stored only in your device's secure storage. We never receive it.

When — and only when — you use this feature, your question and the scripture passages you have selected are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you choose:

  • Anthropic — see Anthropic's Privacy Policy
  • OpenRouter — see OpenRouter's Privacy Policy

Scribe does not run a server in between. We do not see, log, or store these requests or their responses. Once your data reaches the provider you selected, it is handled under that provider's privacy policy and your agreement with them. If you never use AI Research, no data ever leaves your device. The providers you may choose (Anthropic, OpenRouter) maintain their own privacy policies that provide protections equivalent to those described here; review them at the links above before using AI Research.

5. Children's privacy

Scribe is suitable for users of all ages. Because we collect no personal data, we collect none from children either.

6. International users (GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws)

Privacy laws such as the EU GDPR and California's CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data a company holds about you. Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing on our side to access, export, or delete. The data stored on your device is entirely under your control and can be removed by deleting the app.

7. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will revise the effective date shown above and post the new version at this address.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email dev@aiconsult.co.nz.
